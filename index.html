About our school

Strong roots, open skies.

Our school takes its name from the mvule, the great hardwood tree of East Africa. It grows slowly, stands strong, and gives shade to everyone beneath it. That is the kind of growth we hope for in every child.

We follow the Tanzanian national curriculum, enriched with reading programmes, hands-on science and the arts, so pupils leave Standard VII confident, kind and ready for secondary school.

“A child who reads with joy will learn anything they set their mind to.”Mrs. Neema Mushi, Head Teacher

Academics

Three stages, one journey.

Each stage builds on the last, so children move from play-based discovery to confident, independent learning.

Ages 4 – 6

Pre-Primary

Learning through play, stories and song. Children build early literacy, numbers and the social skills to thrive in Standard I.

  • Phonics
  • Early maths
  • Play & movement
Standard I – III

Lower Primary

Fluent reading and writing in English and Kiswahili, strong arithmetic and a first taste of science through experiments.

  • Reading levels
  • Kiswahili
  • Science
Standard IV – VII

Upper Primary

Deeper subject teaching, the Standard IV national assessment and focused preparation for the PSLE in Standard VII.

  • SFNA prep
  • PSLE prep
  • ICT & coding
